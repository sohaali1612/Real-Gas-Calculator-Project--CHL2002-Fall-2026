Real-gas calculator

PVT behavior, enthalpy, entropy and pure-component fugacity of gases from the generic cubic equation of state (van der Waals, Redlich/Kwong, Soave/Redlich/Kwong, Peng/Robinson). Equations and data follow Smith, Van Ness, Abbott & Swihart, 8th ed.

Notes

H and S are relative to the ideal gas at 298.15 K and 1 bar (H = S = 0). Phase “auto” returns the root of lowest Gibbs energy; “vapor” and “liquid” return the largest and smallest root. Check: n-butane, 500 K, 50 bar, PR gives Z = 0.6909, HR = −4985 J/mol, SR = −7.421 J/(mol K); the textbook (Table 13.7) lists 0.6907, −4988 and −7.426. Cp constants (A, B, C, D) are those of Table C.1 and are valid from 298 K upward.
